Synthesize the past week.

1. Read all `daily/` notes from the last 7 days: sum Study hours, count Exercise days, average Mood
2. Read `wiki/log.md` entries from this week — which subjects moved, which stalled
3. Skim `brain/North Star.md` and rate alignment: is time going where the goals point?
4. Check `thinking/` for scratchpads that should be promoted to proper notes or deleted

Present:
- **Week in numbers**: study hours total, exercise days, mood trend, notes created/touched
- **What moved**: subjects/projects that advanced (with wiki page links)
- **What stalled**: North Star focus areas with no activity
- **Patterns noticed**: across dailies and sessions (file genuinely recurring ones in `[[Patterns]]`)
- **Next week**: 2–3 suggested priorities grounded in North Star
