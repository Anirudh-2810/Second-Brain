SLIDE 2: INTRODUCTION TO DERMAL LAYERS
=================================================================

**Main Title:** Dermal Layer Anatomy
**Subtitle:** The Three-Tier Protective System

### Core Concepts:
1. **Epidermis** - The outermost protective barrier
   - Stratified squamous epithelium
   - 5 sub-layers: Basal, Spiny, Granular, Lucid, Corneous
   - Keratinized, avascular, no nerves

2. **Dermis** - The structural and functional core
   - Papillary layer - thin, delicate, finger-like projections
   - Reticular layer - thick, dense connective tissue
   - Contains blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, sweat glands

3. **Hypodermis** - The insulating and energy reserve layer
   - Composed of loose connective tissue and adipocytes
   - Anchors skin to underlying muscles
   - Contains fat stores and thermoregulation structures
