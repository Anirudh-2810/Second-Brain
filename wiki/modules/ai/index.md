## Start Here

- **[[AI_MASTER_NOTES]]** — Master notes covering all 6 modules (syllabus map, tables, diagrams, quick-revision summary).
- **[[sub-notes/MODULE_1_AI_FOUNDATIONS_AGENTS]]** — Detailed sub-notes: AI definitions & history, AI vs human intelligence, AI⊇ML⊇DL⊇NLP, agent architecture, PEAS framework + examples, task-environment properties, the 5 agent types, practice questions.
- **[[sub-notes/MODULE_2_PROBLEM_SOLVING_SEARCH]]** — Detailed sub-notes: problem formulation, BFS/DFS/DLS/IDDFS/UCS, heuristics & admissibility, A* + AO*, minimax & alpha-beta pruning, CSP backtracking & AC-3, search-strategy guide, practice questions.
- **[[sub-notes/MODULE_3_KNOWLEDGE_REPRESENTATION_LOGIC]]** — Detailed sub-notes: propositional logic & FOL, NL→FOL conversion, knowledge engineering, modus ponens/tollens, resolution refutation & CNF, unification, forward/backward chaining, semantic networks, frames, CD theory, practice questions.
- **[[sub-notes/MODULE_4_FUZZY_LOGIC]]** — Detailed sub-notes: crisp vs fuzzy sets, membership functions, fuzzy operations, linguistic variables & hedges, FIS architecture, Mamdani vs Sugeno, defuzzification, 2 complete worked examples, applications, practice questions.
- **[[sub-notes/MODULE_5_PLANNING_NEURAL_NETWORKS]]** — Detailed sub-notes: STRIPS, goal stack planning (worked), partial-order planning; perceptron math, activation functions, MLP, forward propagation, full backprop derivation, gradient descent, worked examples, practice questions.
- **[[sub-notes/MODULE_6_GENETIC_ALGORITHMS]]** — Detailed sub-notes: GA lifecycle, encodings, fitness, selection/crossover/mutation, elitism, termination, schema theory, 2 worked examples ($x^2$ & knapsack), applications, practice questions.

## Reading Order

1. **AI_MASTER_NOTES** — scan the full syllabus (1–2 hrs).
2. Pick a module → read its **sub-notes** for depth.
3. Attempt the **Practice Questions** at the end of each sub-notes file.
4. Cross-check with the video sections: Introduction · Intelligent Agents · Problem Solving · Game Playing · Knowledge Representation · Planning · NLP · Neural Network · Fuzzy Logic · Genetic Algorithm.
