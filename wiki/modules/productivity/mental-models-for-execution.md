# Mental Models for High-Velocity Execution

> **Source:** [[raw-sources/productivity-and-system]] (section 5).

Decision heuristics that concentrate energy on high-yield work and remove the failure modes that waste attention.

---

## 1. The Four Models

### Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)
80% of output flows from 20% of inputs. Focus energy exclusively on the **high-yield leverage points**. In a knowledge base, that means prioritizing the few modules that unlock the most understanding.

### Eisenhower Matrix
```
                 URGENT              NOT URGENT
      ┌─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┐
  IMPORTANT  │  DO IMMEDIATELY    │  SCHEDULE ★        │
             │                    │  (Deep Work lives) │
      ├─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┤
NOT IMPORTANT │  DELEGATE/AUTOMATE │  ELIMINATE         │
      └─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┘
```
- **Urgent & Important → Do immediately.**
- **Important & Not Urgent → Schedule.** — *Deep work lives in this cell* (see [[deep-work-attention-economics]]). The PPO / Black–Scholes / backtest write-ups are exactly this cell.
- **Urgent & Not Important → Delegate / Automate.**
- **Neither → Eliminate.**

### Parkinson's Law
Work expands to fill the time allotted for it. Use **artificial time boxes** (e.g., 90-minute hyper-focused sprints) to enforce maximum velocity.

### Inversion (Carl Jacobi)
Instead of asking "How do I make today productive?", ask **"What would completely ruin today's focus?"** — and systematically remove those failure modes. This is the origin of the "shallow-work prevention" checklist.

---

## 2. Connections to the Module System

- The 90-minute time box pairs with the 4 Deep Work disciplines ([[deep-work-attention-economics]]) — Parkinson's Law turns a calendar block into a deadline, and the Scoreboard tracks the resulting sprints.
- The "Important & Not Urgent" cell holds the long-form study track [[learning-roadmap-and-study-plan]]. Scheduling that cell is what GTD's **Organize** stage performs ([[gtd-task-management]]).
- 80/20 prioritization tells *which* modules to ingest first — a pre-filter for [[pkm-code-framework]] Capture.
- Inversion is a cognitive-quality gate: removing failure modes reduces the "shallow optimizing" risk flagged in [[model-selection-and-model-risk]] and the [[event-driven-backtesting]] look-ahead discipline.
