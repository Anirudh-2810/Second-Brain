# Deep Work & Attention Economics

> **Source:** [[raw-sources/productivity-and-system]] (section 1).

Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push cognitive capabilities to their limit. Deep work creates new value, improves skill, and is hard to replicate — the scarce resource in an attention economy.

---

## 1. Core Definition

- **Deep Work** — Professional activity performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that pushes cognitive capabilities to their limit. Creates new value, improves skill, is hard to replicate.
- **Shallow Work** — Non-cognitively demanding, logistical tasks performed while distracted. Low value, easily replicated (email, meetings, admin).
- **Attention Residue** — When switching from Task A to Task B, attention does not immediately follow; a *residue* stays stuck on Task A, degrading performance on Task B. Context-switching is expensive.

---

## 2. The 4 Deep Work Execution Disciplines

1. **Focus on the Wildly Important** — Set clear daily high-impact goals.
2. **Act on Lead Measures** — Track *time spent in deep focus* (a lead measure), not output (a lag measure).
3. **Keep a Compelling Scoreboard** — Log daily deep work hours visibly.
4. **Create a Cadence of Accountability** — Weekly review of deep work metrics.

> These disciplines are domain-agnostic — they apply directly to long-form study tracks like the [[learning-roadmap-and-study-plan]].

## 3. Where Deep Work Lives

- Per the [[mental-models-for-execution]], deep work sits in the Eisenhower Matrix cell **Important & Not Urgent → Schedule**. If it is not scheduled, it never happens.
- Newton's "Wildly Important" goals and the Weekly Review cadence map cleanly onto the [[gtd-task-management]] **Reflect → Engage** loop.
- Skill acquisition from deep work compounds through [[atomic-habits-systems]] — make the deep-work block daily, obvious, and satisfying.
