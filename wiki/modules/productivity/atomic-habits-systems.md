# Systems & Atomic Habits

> **Source:** [[raw-sources/productivity-and-system]] (section 3), James Clear — *Atomic Habits*.

You do not rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems. Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement — small, repeatable behaviors wired to identity.

---

## 1. Identity-Based Habits

- Focus on **who you want to become**, not what you want to achieve.
- Every action is a vote for the type of person you wish to be; goals give direction, systems make progress inevitable.

---

## 2. The Four Laws of Behavior Change

| Law | Mechanism | Concrete tactic |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Make it Obvious** | Cue visibility | Implementation Intentions *("I will [BEHAVIOR] at [TIME] in [LOCATION]")* and **Habit Stacking** *("After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT]")* |
| 2. **Make it Attractive** | Desire | **Temptation bundling** — pair an action you *want* to do with an action you *need* to do |
| 3. **Make it Easy** | Friction | **2-Minute Rule** — scale down new habits so they take under two minutes to start |
| 4. **Make it Satisfying** | Reward | Immediate reinforcement + visual **habit tracking** (streaks) |

> The four laws run in a loop (cue → craving → response → reward). Break a bad habit by *inverting* the law (make cues invisible, unattractive, difficult, unsatisfying).

---

## 3. Application to the Study System

- **Daily study block as a stack**: "After morning coffee, I open one module page for 2 minutes" exploits the 2-Minute Rule and habit stacking to protect deep-focus time ([[deep-work-attention-economics]]).
- The **Compelling Scoreboard** of deep-work discipline and **visual habit tracking** are the same feedback mechanism (make it satisfying).
- Identity framing aligns with the discipline required by the quant study track ([[learning-roadmap-and-study-plan]]): become *a person who works the frontier*, not just *a person who passes a course*.
- Note-honing loops in [[pkm-code-framework]] are habits too — progressive summarization embedded in a daily routine.
