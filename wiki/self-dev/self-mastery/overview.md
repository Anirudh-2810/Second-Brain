## 3. The Five Cross-Cutting Laws (repeated in every training)

1. **Belief engineers biology and reality before action does.** Placebo/nocebo are measurable; the belief is the input, reality the lagged output (Trainings 1, 2, 20's thermostat).
2. **Identity is the control surface.** You cannot outperform your self-image; identity = thermostat. Change identity first, then results adjust — the opposite of how most people try (Trainings 6, 8, 20, 26).
3. **Attention is the collapse mechanism.** Every version of you already exists (superposition); sustained attention collapses which one shows up. RAS filters ~99.999% of reality (Trainings 3, 5, 16).
4. **Frequency coherence beats intensity.** Desire→belief→behavior→field must be aligned; leaks broadcast scarcity; coherence is the whole manifestation game. "Frequency beats intensity: 10 min/day of rehearsal beats one weekend of hype" (Trainings 4, 5, 10, 14).
5. **Execution is boring physics, not willpower.** Consistency (repetition), boredom tolerance, compression of feedback cycles, and subtraction produce results; motivation/effort economies fail (Trainings 19, 22–26).
