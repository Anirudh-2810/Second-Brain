# 101 Ways to Get More Productive (Shelley Fishel)

> **Source:** [[raw-sources/_extracted/101-ways-to-get-more-productive-shelley-fishel.txt]] (Shelley Fishel, *101 Ways to Get More Productive*, The IT Training Surgery).

101 practical workplace tactics organized by phase: **before work → during work → don'ts during work → after work → general → home-based**. Where Babauta and Young build philosophy, this is the *field manual* — low-tech ergonomics, hygiene, and environment defaulting.

---

## 1. Before Work (prepare → "fortune favours the prepared mind")

- **Make today's task list** for the day; **check the agenda** for the next few weeks.
- **Prepare all materials** before starting (no running to fetch supplies mid-flow).
- **Prime the environment**: proper workspace setup, tidy up, check tools/equipment, distribute data/supplies early.
- **Silence the phone** (silent/vibrate, neutral short ringtones, work/personal SIM split, email filters).
- **Prepare your mind and body**: hearty breakfast, prepare everything for tomorrow the night before, proper mindset. **Sent out tomorrow's work today** so teams can plan ahead.

> Maps to the [[productive-flowchart]] AUDIT/CAPTURE front end, and Babauta's "morning quiet" ([[focus-minimalism-babauta]]).

---

## 2. During Work (execute)

- **Check inboxes regularly, not frequently** — set notification sounds and check at intervals.
- **Focus; one task at a time** — the cognitive core is single-stream; multitasking invites errors (consistent with Babauta & Newport, [[focus-minimalism-babauta]]).
- **Use shortcuts** (keystrokes, hotkeys) to cut command overhead.
- **Get it right first time, every time** — quality-in-process beats rework; productivity = quality × completion.
- **Exploit prime time** — identify your peak-performance window and do the hardest work there (Young's top-heavy scheduling, [[little-book-productivity-scott-young]]).
- **Take notes** — offload from working memory; reminders to phone > internal RAM.
- **Complete communication tasks early** — unblocks the team; respond promptly.
- **Manage the digital surface**: close non-work tabs/windows, bookmark & organize useful sites, minimize open apps, save regularly, keep antivirus/definitions current, backups + backup logs, records storage, regular PC maintenance.
- **Prioritize** as needed; **don't procrastinate**; update schedules/agendas as changes occur; maintain good relationships at work; tackle everything in the meeting (batch questions/decisions, schedule meetings Thu/Fri not Mon).

---

## 3. Don'ts During Work (protect the environment)

- **No entertainment** during work time and **no self-distraction**; **don't let others distract you**; no unplanned "drop-by" meetings (politely reschedule).
- **No downloading/installing with abandon**, no streaming on company bandwidth, no non-work websites, **no socializing during work** (secure the momentum).

> These are [[focus-minimalism-babauta]]'s "clear distractions" codified as office hygiene — the system-level version of Newport's shallow-work rules ([[deep-work-attention-economics]]).

---

## 4. After Work (close the loop)

- **Put away tools, shut down the computer** (full shutdown = fresh memory), tidy up, lock up.
- **Review the day** — analyze how it went, identify a better process, adapt.
- **Pat yourself on the back** — self-encouragement sustains the habit loop (satisfying end-state per [[atomic-habits-systems]] ["Make it Satisfying"]).

---

## 5. General & Home-Based Workers

- **Unwind after work**, music, rise early, don't drink stress away, maintenance on everything, check expiry/inventory, exercise, R&R, sleep, be comfortable. **Find time to exercise** and **get enough sleep** — energy fuels.
- **Honest productivity**: "quantity or quality?", **don't burn yourself out** ("If you are not up to it, STOP"), **get a change of scenery**, **suit yourself**.
- **Home-based**: establish a routine; know when you're most ready; plan ahead; itemize workload; **tell friends/family you're in work mode**; track progress; dedicated home office; outsource; buddy checks; invest in reliable tech; frequent short breaks; dress for the occasion.

> Recurrence: **R&R, breaks, and sleep are scored as productivity**, not lost time — the body-centered pillar ([[overview#Pillar-2]]).

## 6. Categorized Key (the 101 in one line each — pattern scan)

| Phase | Theme | Representative items |
|---|---|---|
| Before | Prepare | task list, agenda, materials, silent phone, breakfast, night-before prep |
| During | Execute | interval inbox, single-task, shortcuts, prime time, notes, early comms, prioritize |
| During (don't) | Protect | no entertainment, no self/other distractions, no unofficial meetings |
| After | Close | tidy, shutdown, review, self-praise |
| General | Sustain | unwind, exercise, sleep, breaks, no burnout |
| Remote | Self-manage | routine, work-mode boundaries, dedicated space, tracking, outsourcing |
