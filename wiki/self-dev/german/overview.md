# German A1 — Overview & Roadmap

> **A1 = "Breakthrough"** — the very first level of the Common European Framework (CEFR). At A1 you can introduce yourself, order food, ask for directions, talk about daily life, and understand slow, clear German. This module is your complete, beginner-friendly guide.

---

## What you'll be able to do at A1

- Greet people and introduce yourself / others
- Talk about where you live, your family, your job, your hobbies
- Order food & drinks, go shopping, ask prices
- Ask for and give directions
- Understand and use basic numbers, times, dates
- Introduce yourself and fill in forms (Goethe Start Deutsch 1 test)

## The Goethe A1 exam (Start Deutsch 1)

4 parts, all on everyday topics. **You need ≥ 60% overall.**

| Part | What you do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Hören** (Listening) | 3 sections: phone messages, announcements, radio conversation | ~20 min |
| **Lesen** (Reading) | 3 sections: signs/emails, match texts to pictures, classify info | 25 min |
| **Schreiben** (Writing) | Fill in a form + write a short message/email (~30 words) | 20 min |
| **Sprechen** (Speaking) | Introduce yourself, spell a word, ask & answer 2 questions, request/make requests | 15 min |

**Exam topics (memorize vocabulary for these):** personal information · family & friends · work · free time & hobbies · food & drink · shopping · travel & traffic · health · education · everyday life · housing · weather.

## Quick-start checklist

- [ ] Learn the alphabet + all sounds (umlauts & ß) — week 1
- [ ] Memorize **sein** and **haben** completely
- [ ] Learn der/die/das for the 200 most common nouns (learn gender WITH the noun!)
- [ ] Learn the 8 most useful verbs: sein, haben, machen, gehen, kommen, essen, trinken, lernen
- [ ] Do 1 Nicos Weg episode per day
- [ ] Practice introducing yourself out loud (exam part 1)
