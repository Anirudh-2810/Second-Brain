## 🎯 Physical Chemistry

### 1. Atomic Structure
**Key Concepts:** Bohr model, quantum numbers, wave-particle duality, Heisenberg uncertainty, Schrödinger equation, orbital shapes, Aufbau principle, Hund's rule, Pauli exclusion, periodic trends.

**Critical Formulas:**
- de Broglie: $\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{mv}$
- Heisenberg: $\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \ge \frac{h}{4\pi}$
- Bohr radius: $r_n = n^2 a_0$ ($a_0 = 0.529$ Å)
- Energy: $E_n = -\frac{13.6 Z^2}{n^2}$ eV
- Rydberg: $\frac{1}{\lambda} = R Z^2 (\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2})$
- Quantum numbers: $n=1,2,3...$; $l=0,1,...,n-1$; $m_l = -l...+l$; $m_s = \pm 1/2$

**JEE Traps:** Nodes = $n-l-1$ radial + $l$ angular; d-orbital splitting (t2g, eg); magnetic moment $\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}$ BM

### 2. Chemical Bonding
**Key Concepts:** Ionic/covalent/metallic/H-bonding, VSEPR, hybridization, MOT, Fajan's rules, dipole moment, bond order, resonance.

**Critical Formulas:**
- VSEPR: $sp$ (2, linear), $sp^2$ (3, trigonal planar), $sp^3$ (4, tetrahedral), $sp^3d$ (5, trigonal bipyramidal), $sp^3d^2$ (6, octahedral)
- MOT: Bond order = $\frac{N_b - N_a}{2}$; $N_b$=bonding e⁻, $N_a$=antibonding e⁻
- Dipole moment: $\mu = q \times d$ (Debye)
- Fajan's: High charge + small cation + large anion $\to$ covalent character
- % ionic character = $16|\chi_A - \chi_B| + 3.5|\chi_A - \chi_B|^2$ (Hannay-Smith)

**JEE Traps:** $O_2$ paramagnetic (MOT), $CO$ bond order 3, $NO$ bond order 2.5, $SO_2$ bent, $XeF_2$ linear

### 3. States of Matter
**Key Concepts:** Gas laws, kinetic theory, real gases (van der Waals), critical constants, liquefaction, liquid state (surface tension, viscosity), solid state (crystal systems, defects).

**Critical Formulas:**
- Ideal gas: $PV = nRT$
- van der Waals: $(P + \frac{an^2}{V^2})(V - nb) = nRT$
- Critical: $V_c = 3b$, $P_c = \frac{a}{27b^2}$, $T_c = \frac{8a}{27Rb}$
- Kinetic: $PV = \frac{1}{3}mn\bar{c}^2$, $KE_{avg} = \frac{3}{2}RT$
- Graham's: $\frac{r_1}{r_2} = \sqrt{\frac{M_2}{M_1}}$
- Compressibility: $Z = \frac{PV}{nRT}$

### 4. Thermodynamics
**Key Concepts:** System/surroundings, state functions, 1st/2nd/3rd laws, enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs free energy, spontaneity, Hess's law, Born-Haber cycle.

**Critical Formulas:**
- 1st Law: $\Delta U = q + w$; $w = -P_{ext}\Delta V$ (rev: $w = -nRT\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1}$)
- Enthalpy: $H = U + PV$, $\Delta H = \Delta U + \Delta n_g RT$
- Hess's Law: $\Delta H_{rxn} = \sum \Delta H_{products} - \sum \Delta H_{reactants}$
- 2nd Law: $\Delta S_{universe} \ge 0$; $\Delta S = \frac{q_{rev}}{T}$
- Gibbs: $G = H - TS$, $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$; spontaneous if $\Delta G < 0$
- $\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K_p = -2.303 RT \log K_c$
- Kirchhoff: $\Delta H_2 - \Delta H_1 = \int \Delta C_p dT$

### 5. Chemical Equilibrium
**Key Concepts:** Law of mass action, $K_p/K_c$, Le Chatelier's principle, degree of dissociation, reaction quotient.

**Critical Formulas:**
- $K_c = \frac{[C]^c[D]^d}{[A]^a[B]^b}$, $K_p = K_c (RT)^{\Delta n_g}$
- $Q < K$: forward; $Q > K$: reverse; $Q = K$: equilibrium
- Le Chatelier: Pressure $\uparrow$ favors fewer gas moles; Temp $\uparrow$ favors endothermic
- van't Hoff: $\ln \frac{K_2}{K_1} = -\frac{\Delta H^\circ}{R}(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1})$

### 6. Ionic Equilibrium
**Key Concepts:** Acids/bases (Arrhenius, Brønsted, Lewis), pH, buffer solutions, hydrolysis, solubility product, common ion effect.

**Critical Formulas:**
- $pH = -\log[H^+]$, $pOH = -\log[OH^-]$, $pH + pOH = 14$ (25°C)
- $K_w = [H^+][OH^-] = 10^{-14}$
- Weak acid: $[H^+] = \sqrt{K_a C}$ (if $C/K_a > 400$)
- Buffer (Henderson-Hasselbalch): $pH = pK_a + \log\frac{[Salt]}{[Acid]}$
- $K_{sp} = [M^{n+}]^m [X^{m-}]^n$; precipitation if ionic product $> K_{sp}$

### 7. Electrochemistry
**Key Concepts:** Conductance, Kohlrausch's law, Nernst equation, electrochemical series, corrosion, batteries, fuel cells.

**Critical Formulas:**
- $\Lambda_m = \frac{\kappa \times 1000}{C}$; $\Lambda_m^\circ = \Lambda_m^\circ(cation) + \Lambda_m^\circ(anion)$
- Nernst: $E = E^\circ - \frac{0.0591}{n} \log Q$ (25°C)
- $E^\circ_{cell} = E^\circ_{cathode} - E^\circ_{anode} = E^\circ_{RP} - E^\circ_{RP}$ (both reduction)
- $\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{cell}$; $\Delta G = -nFE_{cell}$
- Faraday: $m = ZIt$; $Z = \frac{M}{nF}$

### 8. Chemical Kinetics
**Key Concepts:** Rate law, order, molecularity, integrated rate equations, half-life, Arrhenius equation, catalysis.

**Critical Formulas:**
- Rate = $k[A]^x[B]^y$; Order = $x+y$
- Zero order: $[A] = [A]_0 - kt$, $t_{1/2} = \frac{[A]_0}{2k}$
- 1st order: $\ln\frac{[A]_0}{[A]} = kt$, $t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k}$
- 2nd order: $\frac{1}{[A]} - \frac{1}{[A]_0} = kt$, $t_{1/2} = \frac{1}{k[A]_0}$
- Arrhenius: $k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$, $\ln\frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{E_a}{R}(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2})$

### 9. Solid State
**Key Concepts:** Crystal systems, Bravais lattices, packing efficiency, defects, electrical/magnetic properties, band theory.

**Critical Formulas:**
- Packing efficiency: FCC/CCP/HCP = 74%, BCC = 68%, Simple cubic = 52.4%
- Density: $\rho = \frac{Z \cdot M}{N_A \cdot a^3}$
- Void relations: Octahedral void $= 0.414r$, Tetrahedral $= 0.225r$
- Defects: Schottky (pair), Frenkel (interstitial), Non-stoichiometric

### 10. Solutions
**Key Concepts:** Concentration terms, colligative properties, Raoult's law, van't Hoff factor, abnormal molar mass.

**Critical Formulas:**
- Molality: $m = \frac{n_{solute}}{kg_{solvent}}$
- Molarity: $M = \frac{n_{solute}}{L_{solution}}$
- Mole fraction: $x_A = \frac{n_A}{n_A+n_B}$
- $\Delta T_b = i K_b m$, $\Delta T_f = i K_f m$, $\Pi = i CRT$
- $i = 1 + \alpha(n-1)$ (dissociation), $i = 1 - \alpha(1-\frac{1}{n})$ (association)

### 11. Surface Chemistry
**Key Concepts:** Adsorption (physisorption/chemisorption), isotherms (Langmuir, Freundlich), catalysis, colloids, emulsions.

**Critical Formulas:**
- Langmuir: $\frac{x}{m} = \frac{a k P}{1 + k P}$ or $\frac{P}{x/m} = \frac{1}{ak} + \frac{P}{a}$
- Freundlich: $\frac{x}{m} = k P^{1/n}$; $\log\frac{x}{m} = \log k + \frac{1}{n}\log P$

## 🎯 Organic Chemistry

### 1. General Organic Chemistry (GOC)
**Key Concepts:** Inductive effect, resonance, hyperconjugation, electromeric effect, acid/base strength, carbocation/carbanion/free radical stability, reaction intermediates.

**Critical Orders:**
- -I: $NO_2 > CN > F > COOH > Cl > Br > I > OH > C_6H_5 > CH_3$
- +I: $CH_3CH_2^- > (CH_3)_2CH^- > (CH_3)_3C^-$
- Carbocation stability: $3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ > CH_3^+$ (allyl/benzyl > 3°)
- Carbanion stability: $CH_3^- > 1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ$ (opposite of carbocation)
- Acid strength (carboxylic): e⁻ withdrawing $\uparrow$ acidity; e⁻ donating $\downarrow$ acidity

### 2. Isomerism
**Key Concepts:** Structural (chain, position, functional, metamerism, tautomerism), stereoisomerism (geometrical, optical), R/S, E/Z, diastereomers, meso compounds, racemic mixtures.

**Critical Rules:**
- Cahn-Ingold-Prelog: Atomic number priority
- R/S: Lowest priority away $\to$ clockwise = R, anticlockwise = S
- E/Z: High priority same side = Z, opposite = E
- Meso: Plane of symmetry, optically inactive
- Number of stereoisomers: $2^n$ (no symmetry), $2^{n-1}$ (meso present)

### 3. Hydrocarbons
**Key Concepts:** Alkanes (free radical substitution), alkenes (electrophilic addition), alkynes (nucleophilic addition), aromatic electrophilic substitution.

**Critical Reactions:**
- Markovnikov: H adds to less substituted C
- Anti-Markovnikov (peroxide): H adds to more substituted C
- Ozonolysis: $R_2C=CR_2 \xrightarrow{O_3} 2 R_2C=O$
- Hydrogenation: $alkene \xrightarrow{H_2/Pd} alkane$
- Aromatic substitution: Nitration, Sulfonation, Halogenation, Friedel-Crafts

### 4. Alkyl Halides
**Key Concepts:** $S_N1/S_N2$, $E_1/E_2$, elimination vs substitution, rearrangement, Walden inversion.

**Critical Factors:**
- $S_N2$: Primary > Secondary; strong nucleophile; polar aprotic solvent; inversion
- $S_N1$: Tertiary > Secondary; weak nucleophile; polar protic solvent; racemization
- $E_2$: Strong base, anti-periplanar; Hofmann (less substituted) vs Zaitsev (more substituted)
- $E_1$: Carbocation formation, rearrangement possible

### 5. Alcohols, Phenols, Ethers
**Key Concepts:** Preparation, reactions (oxidation, dehydration, esterification), phenols (acidity, electrophilic substitution), ethers (cleavage).

**Critical Reactions:**
- Oxidation: $1^\circ \to$ aldehyde $\to$ acid; $2^\circ \to$ ketone; $3^\circ$ resistant
- Lucas test: $3^\circ$ immediate, $2^\circ$ 5-10 min, $1^\circ$ no reaction
- Phenol acidity: $pK_a \approx 10$; e⁻ withdrawing $\uparrow$ acidity
- Ether cleavage: $R-O-R' + HX \to R-X + R'OH$ (excess HX)

### 6. Carbonyl Compounds (Aldehydes & Ketones)
**Key Concepts:** Nucleophilic addition, oxidation/reduction, condensation, Cannizzaro, Aldol, haloform.

**Critical Reactions:**
- Cyanohydrin: $RCHO + HCN \to RCH(OH)CN$
- Acetal/Ketal: $R_2C=O + 2ROH \xrightarrow{H^+} R_2C(OR)_2$
- Aldol: $\alpha$-H required; $\beta$-hydroxy carbonyl
- Cannizzaro: No $\alpha$-H; disproportionation
- Haloform: $CH_3CO- \xrightarrow{X_2/OH^-} CHX_3 + RCOO^-$
- Tollen's/Fehling's: Aldehydes +ve, ketones -ve

### 7. Carboxylic Acids & Derivatives
**Key Concepts:** Acidity, decarboxylation, HVZ, esterification, acid chloride, anhydride, ester, amide hydrolysis.

**Critical Reactions:**
- Esterification: $RCOOH + R'OH \xrightleftharpoons{H^+} RCOOR' + H_2O$
- Acid chloride: $RCOOH + SOCl_2 \to RCOCl$
- Hydrolysis: Acid chloride > Anhydride > Ester > Amide (reactivity)
- Curtius/Hofmann/Lossen: Rearrangements to amines

### 8. Amines
**Key Concepts:** Basicity, preparation (Gabriel, Hofmann, reduction), diazotization, reactions.

**Critical Points:**
- Basicity: Aliphatic > $NH_3$ > Aromatic (resonance delocalization)
- $pK_b$: $CH_3NH_2 \approx 3.36$, $C_6H_5NH_2 \approx 9.4$
- Diazonium salt: $ArN_2^+Cl^-$; coupling, Sandmeyer, replacement

### 9. Biomolecules
**Key Concepts:** Carbohydrates (monosaccharides, disaccharides, polysaccharides), amino acids, proteins, enzymes, vitamins, nucleic acids.

**Critical Points:**
- Glucose: Aldohexose, cyclic (pyranose), mutarotation
- Sucrose: $\alpha$-Glc + $\beta$-Fru (non-reducing)
- Starch: Amylose + Amylopectin; Cellulose: $\beta$-1,4
- Amino acids: Zwitterion, isoelectric point
- Protein structure: Primary → Secondary (α-helix, β-sheet) → Tertiary → Quaternary

### 10. Polymers
**Key Concepts:** Addition vs condensation, natural vs synthetic, biodegradable, molecular weight.

**Critical Examples:**
- Addition: PE, PP, PVC, PS, PMMA, PTFE
- Condensation: Nylon-6,6, Nylon-6, Polyester (Dacron), Bakelite
- Copolymer: Buna-S, Buna-N
- Rubber: Natural (cis-polyisoprene), Neoprene

### 11. Practical Organic Chemistry
**Key Concepts:** Detection of elements (Lassaigne), functional group tests, purification (distillation, crystallization, chromatography), qualitative analysis.

**Critical Tests:**
- Unsaturation: $Br_2/CCl_4$ (decolorize), $KMnO_4$ (decolorize)
- Carbonyl: 2,4-DNP (yellow ppt), Tollen's/Fehling's
- Carboxylic acid: $NaHCO_3$ (effervescence)
- Phenol: Neutral $FeCl_3$ (violet), $Br_2/H_2O$ (white ppt)
- Amine: Hinsberg test ($1^\circ/2^\circ/3^\circ$), Carbylamine (foul smell)

## 🎯 Inorganic Chemistry

### 1. Periodic Properties
**Key Concepts:** Atomic/ionic radius, ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity, metallic character, diagonal relationships.

**Trends:**
- Radius: $\downarrow$ across period, $\uparrow$ down group
- IE: $\uparrow$ across, $\downarrow$ down
- EA: $\uparrow$ across (generally), $\downarrow$ down
- EN: $\uparrow$ across, $\downarrow$ down

### 2. s-Block (Groups 1, 2)
**Key Concepts:** Alkali/alkaline earth metals, anomalies (Li, Be), compounds (oxides, hydroxides, carbonates, nitrates), biological importance.

### 3. p-Block (Groups 13-18)
**Key Concepts:** B, C, N, O, halogen, noble gas families; oxides, oxyacids, interhalogens, pseudohalides.

**Critical Anomalies:**
- B: Electron deficient, forms $B_2H_6$, $BF_3$
- C: Catenation, allotropes (diamond, graphite, fullerenes)
- N: Triple bond, $N_2$ inert, $NH_3$ basic
- O: Paramagnetic, $O_2$, $O_3$, $H_2O_2$
- Halogens: $F_2$ strongest oxidizer, $I_2$ weakest
- Interhalogens: $XX'$, $XX'_3$, $XX'_5$, $XX'_7$

### 4. d/f-Block (Transition & Inner Transition)
**Key Concepts:** Electronic config, oxidation states, magnetic properties, color, complex formation, catalytic activity, lanthanide/actinide contraction.

**Critical Points:**
- Variable oxidation: Mn (+2 to +7), Cr (+2 to +6)
- Magnetic moment: $\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}$ BM
- Color: d-d transitions, charge transfer
- Lanthanides: +3 common, $4f$ contraction
- Actinides: Radioactive, +3 to +7

### 5. Coordination Compounds
**Key Concepts:** Werner's theory, ligands (mono/bi/polydentate), nomenclature, isomerism (ionization, hydrate, linkage, coordination, geometrical, optical), VBT, CFT, CFSE, spectrochemical series.

**Critical Formulas:**
- CFSE (octahedral): $t_{2g}$: $-0.4\Delta_o$, $e_g$: $+0.6\Delta_o$
- CFSE (tetrahedral): $e$: $-0.6\Delta_t$, $t_2$: $+0.4\Delta_t$; $\Delta_t = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_o$
- Strong field (low spin): $CN^-, CO, NO_2^-$; Weak field (high spin): $H_2O, F^-, Cl^-$
- Chelate effect: Multidentate > monodentate

### 6. Metallurgy
**Key Concepts:** Occurrence, concentration (froth flotation, magnetic, leaching), extraction (roasting, calcination, smelting, electrolysis), refining (distillation, liquation, electrolysis, zone, vapor phase).

**Critical Steps:**
- Oxide ores: Calcination/Roasting $\to$ Reduction (C, CO, Al, electrolysis)
- Sulphide ores: Roasting $\to$ Reduction
- Carbonate ores: Calcination $\to$ Reduction
- Ellingham diagram: $\Delta G^\circ$ vs T for reduction feasibility

### 7. Qualitative Analysis
**Key Concepts:** Cation/anion separation groups, confirmatory tests, flame tests, interference.

**Critical Groups:**
- Group I: $Ag^+, Pb^{2+}, Hg_2^{2+}$ (as chlorides)
- Group II: $Pb^{2+}, Cu^{2+}, Cd^{2+}, Bi^{3+}, Hg^{2+}$ (as sulphides in acidic)
- Group III: $Fe^{3+}, Al^{3+}, Cr^{3+}$ (as hydroxides with $NH_4Cl + NH_4OH$)
- Group IV: $Co^{2+}, Ni^{2+}, Mn^{2+}, Zn^{2+}$ (as sulphides in basic)
- Group V: $Ba^{2+}, Sr^{2+}, Ca^{2+}$ (as carbonates)
- Group 0: $NH_4^+, Mg^{2+}, K^+, Na^+$ (in solution)
