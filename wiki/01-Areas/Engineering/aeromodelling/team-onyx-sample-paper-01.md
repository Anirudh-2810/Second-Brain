# Sample Paper 01 — Hard (40 MCQs, 4 options each)

> Hub: [[INDEX]] · Quick revision: [[team-onyx-round1-revision]] · Theory: [[aerodynamics-foundations]] · [[wings-controls]] · [[avionics-rc-stack]]
> Rules: 40 questions, 1 mark each. No calculator. Target: 32+/40. Time: 45 minutes.

## Section A — Basic Mathematics & Physics (15)

**A1.** An RC plane flying straight and level at 20 m/s drops a practice bomb from 80 m height ($g = 10$ m/s², ignore air drag). After how long does it hit the ground?
(a) 2 s (b) 4 s (c) 8 s (d) 16 s

**A2.** For the drop in A1, how far ahead of the target must the Aux switch be pressed (horizontal range)?
(a) 40 m (b) 60 m (c) 80 m (d) 120 m

**A3.** Air ($\rho = 1.2$ kg/m³) flows over a wing at 60 m/s on top and 40 m/s below, at the same height. The pressure difference and the side with higher pressure are:
(a) 600 Pa, top higher (b) 1200 Pa, bottom higher (c) 2000 Pa, top higher (d) 2400 Pa, bottom higher

**A4.** A plane's engine gives 30 N forward thrust while a crosswind pushes 40 N sideways (perpendicular). The resultant aerodynamic push on the plane is:
(a) 10 N (b) 50 N (c) 70 N (d) 1200 N

**A5.** A 2D rotation-scaling matrix is $\begin{bmatrix}2 & 3 \\\\ 1 & 4\end{bmatrix}$. Its determinant (area scaling factor) is:
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 8 (d) 11

**A6.** A test sled's velocity is $v(t) = 3t^2 - 12t + 9$ m/s. At what time is its acceleration zero?
(a) 1 s (b) 2 s (c) 3 s (d) 4 s

**A7.** An ESC fails with probability 0.1 and the motor fails with probability 0.05, independently. The probability that at least one of the two fails on a flight is:
(a) 0.05 (b) 0.14 (c) 0.145 (d) 0.15

**A8.** A 2 kg model plane speeds up from 10 m/s to 20 m/s. The work its motor must supply (gain in kinetic energy) is:
(a) 100 J (b) 200 J (c) 300 J (d) 450 J

**A9.** A 3S LiPo (11.1 V) drives a motor drawing 20 A. The electrical power delivered is:
(a) 111 W (b) 222 W (c) 333 W (d) 444 W

**A10.** A model plane has mass 4 kg ($g = 10$ m/s²). The lift needed just to hold level flight is:
(a) 4 N (b) 10 N (c) 40 N (d) 400 N

**A11.** Height markers along a test runway read 5 m, 9 m, 13 m, … (arithmetic progression). The 10th marker reads:
(a) 37 m (b) 41 m (c) 45 m (d) 49 m

**A12.** A wing has 30° dihedral. Each half-span is 1 m long. How much higher is each tip than the root?
(a) 0.25 m (b) 0.5 m (c) 0.87 m (d) 1.0 m

**A13.** For $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$, the SUM of the roots is:
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 5 (d) 6

**A14.** A 2200 mAh pack is drained at a steady 11 A. How long until it is empty?
(a) 6 min (b) 12 min (c) 20 min (d) 30 min

**A15.** A plane's airspeed is 25 m/s into a 5 m/s headwind. Its ground speed is 20 m/s. Time to cover 400 m over the ground is:
(a) 13.3 s (b) 16 s (c) 20 s (d) 25 s

## Section B — Aptitude (15)

**B1.** A plane cruises at 60 km/h airspeed into a 10 km/h headwind. Time to cover 150 km over ground is:
(a) 2 h (b) 2.5 h (c) 3 h (d) 3.5 h

**B2.** The same plane returns over the same 150 km with the 10 km/h wind now as a tailwind. Return time is about:
(a) 1.5 h (b) 2 h 9 min (c) 2.5 h (d) 3 h

**B3.** The paper has 40 MCQs and the qualifying mark is 60%. The number of correct answers needed is:
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 26 (d) 30

**B4.** A build team has seniors and juniors in the ratio 3:5, total 40 members. The number of seniors is:
(a) 12 (b) 15 (c) 18 (d) 24

**B5.** Builder A can assemble a wing in 6 hours, builder B in 4 hours. Working together, they finish one wing in:
(a) 2 h 24 min (b) 2 h 40 min (c) 3 h (d) 5 h

**B6.** A propeller bought for ₹800 is sold for ₹920. The profit percent is:
(a) 10% (b) 12% (c) 15% (d) 20%

**B7.** ₹10,000 is invested at 10% per year for 2 years. The difference between compound and simple interest is:
(a) ₹0 (b) ₹100 (c) ₹200 (d) ₹1000

**B8.** The average age of 5 team members is 20 years. A 24-year-old member leaves. The average of the remaining 4 is:
(a) 18 (b) 19 (c) 20 (d) 21

**B9.** Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(a) 36 (b) 40 (c) 42 (d) 44

**B10.** If PROP is coded by letter positions as 16-18-15-16, ROTOR is coded as:
(a) 18-15-20-15-18 (b) 18-15-19-15-18 (c) 17-14-20-14-17 (d) 18-16-20-16-18

**B11.** M is the brother of N. N is the sister of O. O is the father of P. How is M related to P?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Uncle (d) Cousin

**B12.** A plane flies 10 km north, turns right and flies 5 km east, then turns right and flies 10 km south. It is now how far from its start point?
(a) 0 km (b) 5 km east (c) 10 km (d) 15 km

**B13.** A plane flies 150 km out at 50 km/h ground speed and returns over the same route at 70 km/h. Its average speed for the whole trip is:
(a) 55 km/h (b) 58.33 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 62.5 km/h

**B14.** Propeller test-rig gears rotate in cycles of 4, 6 and 8 seconds. All three align every:
(a) 12 s (b) 24 s (c) 36 s (d) 48 s

**B15.** You attempt all 10 aero questions plus 60% of the other 30 questions. Total questions attempted is:
(a) 24 (b) 26 (c) 28 (d) 30

## Section C — Aerodynamics & Avionics (10)

**C1.** A 40 N-weight model is in a steady climb. Its engine gives 30 N thrust against 20 N drag. Which statement is correct?
(a) It descends because drag is less than weight
(b) It climbs at constant speed because lift equals drag
(c) It climbs AND accelerates (lift exceeds weight, thrust exceeds drag)
(d) It flies level and accelerates

**C2.** Glider: rectangular wing, span 12 m, chord 1 m. Delta jet: span 6 m, wing area 24 m². Their aspect ratios and the one with lower induced drag are:
(a) 12 vs 1.5 — glider has lower induced drag
(b) 12 vs 6 — delta has lower induced drag
(c) 6 vs 1.5 — both equal
(d) 12 vs 1.5 — delta has lower induced drag

**C3.** A wing's angle of attack is increased from 5° to 16° to 20°. What happens at 20°?
(a) Lift keeps rising, drag falls — best efficiency point
(b) Flow separation moves forward, a large turbulent wake forms, lift drops and pressure drag jumps (stall)
(c) The airfoil becomes streamlined and drag becomes pure friction
(d) Downwash stops and Newton's third law no longer applies

**C4.** A plane's left aileron jams at neutral while the right aileron still works. Which control is degraded while pitch and yaw stay normal?
(a) Roll — only half the rolling moment remains
(b) Pitch — elevator stops working
(c) Yaw — rudder locks
(d) Thrust — motor cuts off

**C5.** You must build a fast interceptor model (top speed matters most). The best propeller choice is:
(a) 12×4 — big diameter, low pitch, slow spin
(b) 7×6 — small diameter, high pitch, fast spin
(c) 12×8 — big diameter, high pitch on a weak motor
(d) 10×2 — medium diameter, nearly flat blades

**C6.** An 1800 kV brushless motor runs on a 3S pack (11.1 V). Its no-load top speed is about:
(a) 6,000 rpm (b) 12,000 rpm (c) 19,980 rpm (d) 36,000 rpm

**C7.** A 2200 mAh LiPo powers a cruise draw of 22 A. Approximate flight time on one pack is:
(a) 3 min (b) 6 min (c) 10 min (d) 60 min

**C8.** A motor draws 25 A maximum continuously. The correct ESC choice is:
(a) 20 A ESC (cheaper, lighter)
(b) Exactly 25 A ESC (perfect match)
(c) 30–35 A ESC (headroom, runs cool)
(d) 25 A ESC with no BEC (BEC does not matter)

**C9.** A high-wing cargo model is too stable in spiral mode and refuses to turn tightly. The design fix used on real heavies (An-124, Galaxy) is:
(a) Add more dihedral for extra stability
(b) Add slight anhedral to cancel the excess stability
(c) Increase the aspect ratio only
(d) Fit a bigger propeller

**C10.** A new receiver is fitted from a different brand than the transmitter and all servos stay dead, though the battery is full. The most likely cause is:
(a) The propeller pitch is wrong
(b) Protocol mismatch — a receiver binds only to its own brand/type; also verify the 5 V BEC line
(c) Too much dihedral in the wings
(d) The wing has stalled on the ground
