# Expt 4 — Green Synthesis of Biodiesel (Base Catalysis)

**Aim**: synthesize biodiesel (fatty-acid methyl esters, FAME) from vegetable oil with methanol/NaOH.

## 1. Reaction

Transesterification of triglycerides:

$$\mathrm{Triglyceride + 3\,CH_3OH \xrightarrow{NaOH} 3\,FAME\;(biodiesel) + Glycerol}$$

NaOH is a **catalyst** (dissolved in methanol first — "methoxide" solution), not a stoichiometric reagent. Methanol is used in **excess** to drive the reversible equilibrium toward esters.

## 2. Requirements

50 mL food-grade oil (e.g. sunflower), 12 mL methanol, 0.5 g NaOH, distilled water; 250 mL conical flask, water bath, separating funnel, filter + anhydrous $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ for drying.

## 3. Procedure

1. **Catalyst solution**: dissolve 0.5 g NaOH in 12 mL methanol with stirring.
2. **Reaction**: add 50 mL oil; heat 55–60 °C with stirring, 45 min. Never exceed 65 °C (methanol bp — volatile/flammable).
3. **Separation**: transfer to separating funnel, stand undisturbed → upper layer **biodiesel**, lower layer **glycerol**. Drain glycerol.
4. **Washing**: swirl with 15–20 mL warm distilled water, settle, drain aqueous layer; repeat to remove soap/catalyst.
5. **Drying**: shake biodiesel with anhydrous $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$, stand, filter; measure filtrate volume.

## 4. Observations & result

Record: oil appearance, reaction T/t, layer-separation time, biodiesel appearance, washes count, biodiesel volume. Result: volume of biodiesel = ___ mL (yield discussion optional).

## 5. Green-chemistry mapping (viva core)

Renewable feedstock (oil) · catalytic NaOH (principle 9) · 55–60 °C energy efficiency (6) · methanol/water only, no chlorinated solvents (5) · biodegradable, lower-GHG product (10). Biodiesel = alkyl esters of fatty acids from veg oil/animal fat/microalgae via transesterification.

## 6. Precautions

Fume hood; methanol + NaOH handled with care; no overheating; complete layer separation before collection; glycerol/wash water to lab waste stream.
