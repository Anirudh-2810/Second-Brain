# Engineering Biology — Overview

## 1. Why biology in a BTech

Modern engineering problems are bio-problems: glucose biosensors (enzymes + microelectronics), starch bioplastics (materials), penicillin fermentors (bioprocess), genome search (algorithms), epidemic forecasting (dynamical models). The course trains engineers to **read a biological system, quantify it, and build on it**.

## 3. Study strategy

Mechanisms before names (replication fork order; inhibition Km/Vmax logic), one equation per unit ($v_0$ Michaelis–Menten; Monod $\mu$; SIR flows), tables for comparisons (prokaryote/eukaryote; FASTA/BLAST; in-line/on-line/at-line).
