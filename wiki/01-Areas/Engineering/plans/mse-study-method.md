# MSE Study Method — see it, do it, test it

## The loop (one ~50-min block = one unit)

1. **Recall cold (10 min)** — before opening anything, write from memory: definitions, formula, or the self-test question. *This is the studying* — re-reading is only the repair.
2. **Repair (25–30 min)** — open **only** the pack section + its deep page; fix **only what you missed**; write the corrected line.
3. **Re-test (10 min)** — close everything, redo from memory. Still wrong → **flag the unit** for the next spaced-review block.

**Rules that make it work**

- **Hardest subject first.** Maths goes in the first block of the day, while focus is highest (your "bend point" is maths — [[comparing-the-wrong-number-practice]] step 4).
- **Log units, not hours.** End each day with the units covered + one line. No hours anywhere.
- **Spaced review.** Every 3rd day, the *first* block is a cold re-test of prior units; re-read only flagged gaps.
- **Pass gate.** A unit is *done* only when you reproduce its recall or pass its self-test **unprompted**.
- Daily rhythm at ~3–4 h = **3–4 blocks**. Rotate subjects so every day touches at least two.

## 10-day order (16 → 25 Oct)

Scope + order only. Choose the daily units from the table; keep Maths first.

- **Day 1** — U1, U2, U3 (Maths M1, fresh)
- **Day 2** — U4, U5, U6
- **Day 3** — spaced re-test (U1–U6) → U7, U8
- **Day 4** — U9, U10, U11
- **Day 5** — U12, U13, U14 + spaced re-test (U9–U14)
- **Day 6** — U16, U17, U18
- **Day 7** — U19, U20, U21
- **Day 8** — U22, U23, U24 + spaced re-test (Physics)
- **Day 9** — full cold Maths review + build U25 (formula recall card)
- **Day 10** — full cold self-test per subject (dress rehearsal)

**Adjust so the earliest paper is rehearsed last.** Once the exam timetable is known, shift the last 2 days so whichever subject sits first gets its dress-rehearsal 1–2 days before its paper. Do **not** add timing estimates to this page.

## How to use the decks and tests

- **Flashcards** ([[mse-flashcards-am1]] · [[mse-flashcards-chem-m1]] · [[mse-flashcards-physics]]): read the bold question, say the answer aloud, then click the **`Answer`** callout to check. Overdue cards → flag for the next spaced block. No SR plugin is installed, so this is manual — a daily 5-minute pass over the day's units is enough.
- **Recall tests** ([[mse-recall-test-am1]] · [[mse-recall-test-chem-m1]] · [[mse-recall-test-physics]]): attempt **cold, on paper**, then reveal. Score by the score-sheet at the bottom of each test.
- **Log** with [[study-session]] (paste into the daily note) or just the one line.
