# MSE Study Plan — starting 26 Oct 2026

## Objective

Cover the three examined backsets to a self-test-passing level before the MSE window opens:
- **Applied Mathematics-I** — Modules 1 (Matrices), 2 (Partial Differentiation/Jacobian), 3 (Homogeneous Functions). [pattern: [[ise-exam-prep-am1]]]
- **Engineering Chemistry** — Module 1 (Water Technology / Hardness), numerical-heavy. [home: [[module-1-water-technology-hardness]]]
- **Engineering Physics** — Lasers (Module 2) + slit/diffraction theory (Module 1). [packs: [[lasers-quick-ref]] · [[module-1-optics-interference-diffraction]]]

## Day 1 — Maths: Matrices (Module 1) + rank/system core

1. Skim [[mse-revision-am1]] §Module 1, then the deep sections in [[module-1-matrices]].
2. Drill: normal form → rank (Q1-type), consistency of $[A|B]$, eigen/Cayley-Hamilton, orthogonal $Q^{-1}=Q^T$.
3. Self-test: [[quiz-2026-09-22-systems-consistency-rank]] (9 Q) end-to-end.
4. Must-reproduce: the Q1 normal-form matrix from [[ise-exam-prep-am1]].

## Day 2 — Maths: Partial Differentiation + Euler (Modules 2 & 3)

1. [[mse-revision-am1]] §Module 2–3.
2. Deep: [[module-2-partial-differentiation]] (Jacobian, chain rule, maxima/minima) + [[module-3-homogeneous-functions]] (Euler 1st & 2nd order).
3. Worked patterns to reproduce: 3-var Jacobian determinant; $u_{xx}+u_{yy}+u_{zz}$ Laplacian identity; Euler degree check on a $\sin^{-1}$-of-ratio function.
4. Self-test: [[prerequisite-toolkit]] gaps + 3 Jacobian problems from the practice pools.

## Day 3 — Chemistry: Water Technology / Hardness (Module 1)

1. [[mse-revision-chem-m1-water]] top-to-bottom.
2. Numerical drills in order:
   - EDTA: [[edta-numericals-worked-bank]]
   - Zeolite: [[zeolite-process-numericals]] (reproduce 500 · 300 · 15,262.5 L · 187.2 L)
   - Ion-exchange: [[ion-exchange-numericals]] (reproduce IE1–IE8)
3. Units + traps: [[units-of-hardness-industrial-problems-revision]] + [[hard-water-industry-effects]] (boiler corrosion vs caustic embrittlement).
4. Self-test: convert between ppm / °Cl / °Fr / °dH / meq/L without notes.

## Day 4 — Physics: Lasers + Slit Theory

1. [[mse-revision-physics-lasers-slit]] Part A → deep [[lasers-quick-ref]].
   - Reproduce numericals (a) $A_{21},B_{21}$ (b) $g_{th}$ (c) mode spacing/count (d) He-Ne λ.
2. Part B → deep [[module-1-optics-interference-diffraction]] + [[thin-film-interference-revision]].
   - Reproduce the thin-film derivation and the bright/dark decision table from memory.
3. Self-test: YDSE vs single-slit vs grating bright/dark without the table.

## Day 5 — Mixed consolidation + weak-spot repair

1. Re-run every self-test cold from Days 1–4; flag anything below pass.
2. Targeted re-read only of flagged topics (deep pages).
3. Write a one-page formula recall card per subject (no peeking).
4. Confirm exam date/time/room per subject — **currently unknown** (not found in vault); fill in the table below.
