## How to Use This Plan

- **If you miss a lab:** Buffer weeks are W6 and W16 — use them to clear backlogs; write-ups require handwritten algorithms + faculty signature per [[lab-ca-and-experiments#laboratory-experiments--assignments-15-marks]].
- **OST prep (W11):** covers only M1-3 — prioritize `if/switch/loops + arrays/strings`; see [[lab-ca-and-experiments#ost--15-marks]].
- **Quiz prep (W16):** entire syllabus — drill `debug / complete-code / predict-output` per [[assessment-guide-ese-ost-quiz#quiz--15-marks]].
- **Stay-ahead rule (per [[North Star]]):** finish each EXP write-up in its lab week — 20% of the 15 marks is timely submission.
