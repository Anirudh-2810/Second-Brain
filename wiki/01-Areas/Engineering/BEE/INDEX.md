## Exam Strategy (MU pattern)

- **Module 1 + 2 are the numerical backbone** (~50-60% of papers): Thevenin/Norton problems and R-L-C phasor problems appear every year
- Module 3 transformer efficiency/regulation is a guaranteed numerical
- Module 4: derive-and-explain questions (EMF equation, back-EMF significance) + slip numericals
- Module 5 is theory-memory: wiring diagrams + safety lists — cheapest marks in the paper
