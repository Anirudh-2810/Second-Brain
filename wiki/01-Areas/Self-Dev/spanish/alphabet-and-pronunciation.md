# Spanish — Alphabet, Pronunciation & First Contact

## 1. The class-zero trick — 50 words you already know

VideoEle **Primera clase de español** (`A1-Primera-clase-de-espanol.html`) doesn’t start with grammar. It shows **50 cognates** — words almost identical in English/Indian languages: *fiesta, siesta, paella, chico, viva, hotel, taxi, etc.* You deduce meaning → realize you never start from zero → confidence + first phonology ear-training.

> Functional contents: **Saludar y despedirse** (greet/bye), **Preguntar y expresar conocimiento** (ask/say you know). Lexical: 50 cognates. Grammar: letter-sound correspondence, noun phrase order.

**Takeaway:** Before drilling verbs, shadow those 50 words aloud to feel Spanish sounds vs Indian English.

## 2. Pronunciation — marcas & personajes

Two dedicated units:

- **Pronunciación (marcas)** — say international **brands** in Spanish: *Nike, Coca-Cola, Samsung* → Spanish vowels are pure 5 (a,e,i,o,u), no diphthongs; **j** = /x/ (kh), **ll** = /ʝ/, **ñ** = /ɲ/, **h** always silent, **b/v** same.
- **Pronunciación (personajes)** — same for **famous names**: *Beatles, Shakespeare* etc. Trains contrastive listening.

**Spanish vs Indian English quick contrasts:**

| Letter | Spanish | Indian English trap |
|---|---|---|
| **a,e,i,o,u** | 5 pure vowels, never change | a in “cat” vs “father” — Spanish a always /a/ |
| **j** | /x/ kh (Juan = Khuan) | j = дж |
| **h** | silent always (hola = ola) | h = ह |
| **ll** | y (calle = caye) | - |
| **ñ** | ny (España = Espanya) | - |
| **r/rr** | tap vs trill (pero vs perro) | single r |
| **b/v** | same /b/ | b vs v distinct |
| **z, c(e,i)** | /θ/ in Spain (θeta) or /s/ in Latin America | - |

## 3. Alphabet (abecedario) — from “Números y letras”

Unit **Números y letras** (`A1-Numeros-y-letras.html`) puts letters + numbers in street context (car plates, Lotto, pant sizes). Spanish alphabet = 27 letters (includes ñ). Names to know: **eñe** for ñ, **be, ce, de, e, efe, ge, hache, i, jota, ka, ele, eme, ene, eñe, o, pe, cu, erre, ese, te, u, uve, doble ve, equis, ye, zeta**.

> Functional: **Deletrear** (spell), **Identificar cosas**, **Decir número de teléfono**. Lexical: letters, numbers 1-1000, nearby objects. Grammar: alphabet, numbers, *cuánto/a/os*.

## 4. Greetings you asked — hola & buenos días and friends

From Primera clase functional **Saludar y despedirse**:

| Spanish | English | When / note |
|---|---|---|
| **¡Hola!** | Hello / Hi | anytime — the universal |
| **¡Buenos días!** | Good morning | till ~14:00 (lit. “good days”) |
| **¡Buenas tardes!** | Good afternoon / evening | ~14:00 till sunset |
| **¡Buenas noches!** | Good night / good evening (night) | at night |
| **¡Adiós!** | Goodbye | bye |
| **¡Hasta luego!** | See you later | casual bye |
| **¿Cómo estás?** | How are you? (tú, informal) | to friend/classmate |
| **¿Cómo está (usted)?** | How are you? (formal) | to teacher/elders |
| **Bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?** | Fine, thanks. And you? | reply |
| **Encantado/a** | Nice to meet you | encantad**o** (male) / **a** (female) |
| **Por favor / Gracias / De nada** | Please / Thanks / You’re welcome | essentials |

> Your “hol hola” = **Hola**; “beonus dias” = **Buenos días** — now in vault correctly.

## 5. Numbers you’ll hit next

VideoEle splits as **1-20** (`A1-numeros-20.html`) then **20-100** (`A1-numeros-100.html`) then via “Números y letras” up to 1000. Cultural extras: **céntimos de euro**, **medidas**, **matrículas** (plates), **Bonoloto**.

Quick: 1 uno, 2 dos, 3 tres, 4 cuatro, 5 cinco, 6 seis, 7 siete, 8 ocho, 9 nueve, 10 diez, 11 once, 12 doce, 13 trece, 14 catorce, 15 quince, 16 dieciséis, 20 veinte, 30 treinta, 100 cien.

## Practice now (2 min)

1. Say your 50 cognates aloud: *hotel, taxi, fiesta, siesta…*
2. Spell your name: “Me llamo Anirudh. Se escribe A-N-I-R-U-D-H.”
3. Greet yourself: “¡Hola! Buenos días. ¿Cómo estás? Bien, gracias.”
4. Watch VideoEle Primera clase video once with ES subs, once without.
