# Spanish — Practice & Exercises (VideoEle method)

## The 4-step Guía flow (do for each video)

1. **Antes de visionar** — prime topic, skim title + image
2. **Mientras visionas** — watch 3-6 min, answer Guía questions during watch (pause often)
3. **Después de visionar** — do most tasks from memory → replay to fill gaps
4. **Para aprender más** — optional deep dive on one grammar/culture point

Then: **Transcripción PDF** (read + translate) → **Soluciones PDF** (check, learn errors) → **Actividades interactivas** (H5P in browser) → **shadow aloud** 2 min.

## H5P interactives tips

- After video, not before. Replay video as needed to answer.
- Interactive for “En el presente” = 22 gap-fills (present verbs) — type verb correctly with accent.
- If many wrong, rewatch with Spanish subs, then retry.

## 8-week A1 plan (15-20 min/day)

Reuse from [[overview]] — 1: Primera+Pron+Números | 2: Artículos/demostrativos | 3: Países/días/hora | 4: Barrio/tiendas/cuerpo | 5: Comida/familia/casa | 6: Presente/gustar | 7: Ropa/deporte/transporte | 8: Ser-estar/saber-conocer/review + 1 CancionEle song

Track 5-10 new words/day in Anki.

## CancionEle drill

https://videoele.com/CancionEle/index.html — pick 1 song/week: listen + read lyrics → gap-fill → sing along for rhythm + colloquial.

## DELE A1 light prep

If aiming DELE A1 (like German Goethe A1): 4 skills — Comprensión lectura/auditiva, Expresión escrita/oral on everyday topics (personal info, family, shopping, time). VideoEle A1 covers all vocab buckets; add 1 mock from Instituto Cervantes samples in week 8.
