## The protocol (8 steps, in order)

### 1. Audit attention — stuck means misdirected, not broken

Stuck = distracted + directionless: pouring time into inputs that don't move you where you claim to go. Pleasure fakes motion — doomscrolling feels like learning because images keep changing, then you stop and went nowhere. Other people's doubt is a distraction vector: asked "will your clothes really sell?", answer "why not?" and keep marketing. Direction beats goal-fantasy — vague "be a millionaire" does nothing; a road you stay on guarantees arrival. **Drill:** log 2 full days hour-by-hour in a notebook; circle helpful, highlight harmful; name your top-1 distraction. "Time is gone. Like that second that just went by, it's gone." / "Journal. Journal. Journal."

### 2. Declutter outer → stabilize inner

Mess amplifies low mood; order is proof of control. Bed first every morning, then room, then phone — worst case you still won one thing. Declutter the phone like the room: delete 5 unused apps; keep only calendar, calculator, one messenger, one social, music. Real self-care is basic maintenance done intentionally — cold water, face routine, trim as daily check-in. Pick ONE change with best return (his: sleep + schedule — "you can't burn both ends of the candle") and protect 15 min of pure solitude daily. "Support the internal by fixing the external."

### 3. Fix identity and values first

Ask "who do I want to become?" never "how do I feel?" Sad-but-wants-disciplined: can't do 9 tasks, does 1 — mood follows effort. Judge currency by behavior: what do your actions show you value — comfort or time/energy? Name core values, then two-column audit: habits that improve life vs habits that don't; ask what converts the second into the first. Nightly 3-way score: did today move me closer / hold me / push me away? One line of evidence; if not closer, name tomorrow's one closer-action. "forward is not easy, but backwards will cost you everything."

### 4. Engineer friction + replacement — never leave a vacuum

Old habits win because they're familiar and permission-free; the new self has no track record yet. Make bad loops 20 seconds harder (hide snacks deep, log out, delete, relocate) AND install the replacement in the same slot — swap morning phone + Netflix + junk for cold-water splash + run. A removed habit with no substitute invites chaos. "I'm tired of being tired."

### 5. Work in silence

Oversharing makes your life public property and leaks motivation: announced "no more planes this year," then a family emergency forced an Oregon flight — now explanations owed. Never relay unverified secondhand stories — he repeated one, learned "it wasn't what happened," torched credibility. Announcing goals pays premature dopamine; the brain feels done after applause. New defaults: "going outside," "just plan to do good in life." One trusted person gets full honesty; results speak on arrival. 7-day drill: tell no one a current goal; note each urge to announce and its trigger. "Silence is your shield." / "true peace and true growth comes in private."

### 6. Use finitude as fuel

Infinite-life mindset breeds dormancy: phone-stuck, passive, risk-free — "existed but never lived." Scarcity creates value; silent regret accumulates with each untaken risk until heaviness feels unexplained. Compare avatars: who you became vs who you could've been — "can I end as this level-one version?" Fear as compass: if fear arrives first to an opportunity you actually want, go — his unprepared last-minute guitar gig with shaking hands became a blast. 24-hour drill: one wanted thing → smallest A-to-B step doable in 20 minutes → do the B-step within 24h. "You are going to die one day." / "originally my biggest fear was dying and now it's not living"

### 7. Persist through the dip — hardest stretch precedes assembly

Quitting clusters near breakthroughs; "now now now" impatience kills difficult-but-valuable pursuits. Aim center field, not just the dream: "what is between here and there, and what can I do in-between today?" Collapse reframed: lost fake friends → real solitude; brutal breakup → rebuilt from solidest pieces only, then 10x. Signs are fuel, not souvenirs — visions only matter with work (eagle over the Gulf Coast → painted eagle guitar → kept going until life turned gradually, then all at once). Name the door you want to quit at; write 3 center-field moves; do one today. "Action is the antidote."

### 8. Document to stay honest — information only counts applied

Journal to see yourself: what's written can be circled and corrected; what stays in-head loops. Film ordinary life as cinema — morning sun, routine, errands, one human moment — and routine re-enchants; shoot 60–90 seconds weekly, no posting required. Creation careerism is professional people-pleasing (chasing likes burns out the maker); document how you actually live instead. Run process wagers with a friend + stakes — his "81 shots": 3 dailies, 2 weeklies, 1 monthly, 1 end-goal, zero vanity metrics. Review weekly, adjust, keep going. "Information only goes so far."

## Failure modes

| Mode | Looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement dopamine | posting goals, then coasting | §5 quiet build; reveal on completion |
| Vacuum removal | deleting apps with no substitute | §4 replacement in same slot |
| Goal-fantasy rehearsal | vision boards, no road | §1 direction + §7 center-field moves |
| Backward comfort trade | reclaiming old image at full cost | §3 closer/same/farther nightly score |
| Content-as-progress | watching reset videos, changing nothing | §8 apply-then-review loop |
