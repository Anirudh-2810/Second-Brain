## 1. Starters that actually work — interest beats lines

- **Become openable.** Distinctive-but-genuine presentation (style, energy, objects you carry) makes *others* open you — a stranger chased him over flared pants; a woman opened with "whatever it is, I like it."
- **Remark on shared surroundings, humor welcome.** Starving over a delayed steak, he told a fidgety stranger "they must be raising a cow back there" — she laughed, his own tension broke.
- **Notice small specifics.** "I see you got your hair done" made a coworker's day; "did you make this turquoise ring yourself?" unlocked France/Arizona stories. Specific detail > generic praise.
- **Ask opinions, not facts.** "Where from? Like it there? Born there?" dies fast. "How do you feel about it?" activates people — ask any guitarist about strings and you'll get a full lecture.
- **Don't personalize misses; get reps.** Some people won't talk even if they adore you. Older people are easiest practice — they initiate without fear of judgment.

## 2. Depth is a chain — small talk is the on-ramp

- **Accept the interest filter.** Same book topic: one person "uh, is that not interesting?" (move on), another talked 2 hours. Same opener, opposite outcome — receptivity decides.
- **Train reference-hunting.** Any noun → instant associates: car → tires / new-vs-used / road trips. Drill: scan your room, link 5 nouns to 2 associates + 1 question each, aloud, daily.
- **Refuse to skip small talk.** People who won't work through it never reach depth. One small "ping" ("oh, now I can talk about this") cascades into the long conversation they claim to want.
- **Do daily impromptu reps on camera.** Prompt: "if your life was a book, what chapter is this?" His demo: "when winter meets spring" — shedding dead weight before regrowth. ~2/day builds pressure tolerance.
- **Collect proof across people-types.** 20 minutes with an elder on a store aisle recalibrates what rejection means better than any theory.

## 3. Flow — equals, branches, darts

- **De-pedestal the other person.** Sender → receiver → feedback, two humans — not gods. Drop the crush-awe frame before opening.
- **Match directness.** Too tight ("how's your day?" → "good.") starves; too loose (5 questions at once) overwhelms. Try: "how's your day — learn anything new, anything on your mind?"
- **Answer with branches.** Closed: "all is well, how's yours?" Open: "all is well, felt productive today, got a lot done — what about you?" The word "productive" invites the next question.
- **Act through anxiety.** Overthinking compounds; one simple line said aloud beats 10 minutes of perfect-hello brainstorming. Action is the release valve.
- **Ping their interests, not yours.** He asks his dad about football though he no longer watches; dad asks about guitar/travel. Ask a player "what kind, how long?" and you'll get a demo 9 times out of 10.
- **Place over interrogation.** Rapid-fire biography (where from / siblings / favorite color) feels like an interview. Shared context first ("that oak tree, ice-cream guy — cookies and cream, on me — you from around here?"), history later.

## 4. Presence is the platform — put down the phone

- **Protect the sleep edges.** Body restores overnight; a "black box" of stressors first/last thing spikes arousal — wake at 8, scroll with eye-crust in, look up at 2pm.
- **See the belonging swap.** Each scroll gives a pinch of feel-good belonging with no real membership; curated feeds become yes-men halls that erase gray.
- **Notice atrophy.** His checklist: can't point north, can't get home without GPS, can't sit with family phoneless, can't do 8+6 without a calculator.
- **Drop debate-brain.** "I can understand you and not agree." Help offered ("set boundaries") meets "that won't fix everything" — he counters: too much water can kill you; now go a year without it.
- **Install friction.** Phone charges across the room; leave it home on short trips; narrate pickups aloud ("Self, what am I doing here?"); scroll only to hunt inspiration, with an internal off-track timer.
- **Create more than you consume.** Talk, walk, build, record — then log off.

## 5. Drills (pick one per day)

1. **3-opener rep:** 1 specific-detail compliment + 1 surroundings remark + 1 opinion question. Log sentence-back vs word-back.
2. **Chain drill (5 min):** 5 random nouns → 2 associates + 1 follow-up each, aloud + one 60-sec impromptu recording.
3. **Branch answers (3 days):** never one-word "how are you" — template: concrete detail + return question.
4. **48-h presence reset:** no phone 30 min after wake / before sleep, one phoneless outing, narrate every pickup; halve day-2 count.
