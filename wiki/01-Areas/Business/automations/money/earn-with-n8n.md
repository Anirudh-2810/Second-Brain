## Path 1 — Freelance Builds

**What:** custom workflow per client. Typical jobs: Telegram bots, lead routing, report digests, CRM sync, scraping pipelines.

**Steps:**
1. Portfolio: 3 demo videos (quick-start guide Phase 3).
2. Apply to 5–10 relevant Upwork jobs/day with a 3-line personalized proposal + Loom re-creation of their ask.
3. Price first 3 jobs low ($50–150) for reviews, then jump to market rate.

**Rates anchor:** beginners $25–40/hr; solid portfolio $50–80/hr; niche expert (e.g., "AI voice agents") $100–150/hr.

**Secret weapon:** respond to job posts with a *working prototype* built in 30 min from the library. Conversion beats every other freelancer tactic.

## Path 2 — Productized Service + Retainers (recommended endgame)

**What:** fixed-scope packages sold to one niche. Example: "Daily KPI digest + weekly report automation for gyms — $497 setup + $149/mo."

**Why retainers win:** one build pays monthly forever. 10 clients × $199 = ~$2k/mo baseline before any new sales.

**Steps:**
1. Choose niche where you know the painful manual task (see playbook file).
2. Build the flagship workflow ONCE.
3. Sell same build repeatedly with light customization (2–4 h per client).
4. Include monitoring + small changes in retainer; charge separately for new workflows.

## Path 3 — Template Products

**What:** cleaned, documented workflow JSONs sold as packs.

**Where:** Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, your own site, n8n creator program.

**Product ladder:**
- Free lead magnet: 3 simple templates (email capture)
- $19–29: niche pack ("10 Real Estate Agent Automations")
- $79–149: mega pack + video setup course
- $299+: "white-label rights" tier for agencies

**Rules:** include a PDF with screenshots + import instructions; test every export on fresh n8n instance; MIT source library allows commercial sale of derivatives (still write your own docs/branding).

## Path 4 — Micro-SaaS Wrapper

**What:** hide n8n behind a simple web app; users pay subscription. Examples: "AI social post scheduler for dentists", "invoice-chaser SaaS".

**Architecture:** landing page → Stripe checkout → webhook fires your n8n → workflow does the work per tenant → results into their account/email.

**Validation first:** sell the service manually (Path 2) to 5 customers; only productize what people already paid for.

## Path 5 — Content & Education

**What:** YouTube/TikTok shorts of workflow builds, newsletter, cohort course.

**Funnel:** short video showing satisfying automation → free template (email capture) → paid pack (Path 3) → service inquiries (Path 1/2). Every piece feeds the others.

## Path 6 — Use It Inside Employment

Automate tasks at current job → measurable hours saved → promotion case or consulting side-contract with the same company's partners. Lowest risk entry point if you have a day job.
