## 1. Hour map

| Block | Min | What happens | Win condition |

## 4. STAR bank (90s each, number or lesson in every story)

- **Failure:** stock-agent CSV-cast crash — raw text column hit `int()`, backtest died. Fix: try/except with bad-row counter + report. Lesson: validate before concluding.
- **Ownership:** handsens101 jitter — raw landmarks shook, cursor danced. Fix: 0.85 detection confidence + exponential smoothing before actuation. Lesson: filter before acting.
- **Teamwork:** class lab split / friends Vivaan, Noah — owned the parser, shipped the chart the group actually checked.
- **Weakness (non-disqualifying + mitigation):** shallow ML depth today → council reps + quant study plan building it systematically.
- **First-week offer:** event feedback CSV → parser → one chart the council actually checks. Same offer as Onyx thrust-CSV, translated.
