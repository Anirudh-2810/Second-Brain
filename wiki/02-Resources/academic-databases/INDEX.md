## Free Legal Access Ladder (try in order)

1. **KJSCE library e-journals** — the college almost certainly holds IEEE (via AICTE/INDEST-class consortia) and at least one other; ask the library for the proxy/off-campus link
2. **OpenAccess filters** — every DB above has one; filter first when off-campus
3. **arXiv preprints** — q-fin, cs.RO, cs.LG are free and usually the same content as the published version
4. **Unpaywall / OpenAlex browser extensions** — auto-finds legal OA copies
5. **Author copies** — ResearchGate, personal pages, or a polite email; authors almost always say yes

Never use piracy mirrors — the ladder above covers ~90% of papers legally.
